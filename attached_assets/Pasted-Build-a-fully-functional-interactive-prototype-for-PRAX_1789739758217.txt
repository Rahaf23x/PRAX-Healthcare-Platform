Build a fully functional interactive prototype for PRAX – Smart Patient Identification During Ambulance Transport.

PRAX is an emergency healthcare platform that helps paramedics identify patients quickly, retrieve their complete critical medical information, receive AI-assisted clinical alerts, and securely send the case information to a suitable nearby hospital before arrival.

The prototype must support both Arabic and English, with a language switcher. When Arabic is selected, the interface must automatically switch to RTL. When English is selected, it must switch to LTR.

IMPORTANT:
This is a demonstration prototype. Use fictional patient data and simulated fingerprint verification. Do not connect to real medical databases or real patient information.

Display the PRAX logo and the title:

PRAX

--------------------------------------------------

Display a large fingerprint scanner interface in the center of the screen.

Show:

- "Place patient's finger on the scanner"
- "Waiting for fingerprint..."

Add a button:
"Scan Fingerprint"

When clicked, simulate the fingerprint scanning process:

1. Scanning...
2. Verifying identity...
3. Identity verified successfully

Then display a green success message:

"Patient Identity Verified Successfully"

Arabic:
"تم التحقق من هوية المريض بنجاح"

The fingerprint is simulated only for the prototype.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 – ALTERNATIVE IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------

"Alternative Identification Method"

The fingerprint method should be the primary method, while National ID is the backup method.

AI CLINICAL SUPPORT
==================================================

Create a prominent section called:

AI Clinical Support

The AI analyzes the patient's medical information, allergies, medications, conditions, and vital signs.

The AI should generate useful emergency alerts and recommendations.

Example alerts:

CRITICAL ALERT:
"Patient has a documented severe allergy to Penicillin."

MEDICATION WARNING:
"Avoid medications that may interact with the patient's current medications."

CLINICAL ALERT:

The AI must NOT diagnose the patient or make autonomous treatment decisions.
